# Pueblo pilot, step 3 — detail-page and identity audit

Audit date: 2026-10-13. Sources: complete local `type=all` and `type=shopper` snapshots in `data/catalog-pilots/`; 20 live product detail pages in `data/catalog-pilots/pueblo-20261013/detail_audit.json`; Pueblo [FAQ](https://www.puebloweb.com/preguntas-frecuentes) and [terms](https://www.puebloweb.com/terminos). This is a read-only, selected audit, not a random estimate of the entire catalog or an in-store price check.

## Checklist and findings

- Full-feed integrity: 20,175 unique catalog SKUs, 935 unique shopper SKUs, all 935 joined by SKU. There were **zero** missing parsed display prices, names, sizes or duplicate SKUs in either complete snapshot, and **zero** name/pack/display-price differences for the 935 joined SKUs. These counts say nothing about physical-store inventory.
- Stratified detail review: five seeded-random shopper, seven seeded-random catalog-only, and eight edge cases (multi-buy, per-lb, low/high price, last page, leading `*`). All **20/20** detail URLs returned HTTP 200; **20/20** displayed the captured grid price. These results verify this sample, not all 20,175 products.
- **8/20** detail pages had a merchant-labeled `Reg.` price. Seven were shopper examples; the eighth, [Hillandale eggs, SKU 405982](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/405982/hillandale-farms-huevos-medium-eggs), was **not** in the shopper but showed `2/$4.00`, `Reg. $3.29`, `$2.00 c/u`. Another non-shopper example, [Nutra Coco, SKU 123421](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/123421/nutra-coco-100-pura-agua-de-coco), showed `2/$6.00`, `Reg. $3.29`. Therefore `catalog-only` **does not mean regular-priced or not on promotion**. The 324 catalog-only rows with a multi-buy display deserve a separate promotion category; the text `Reg.` is a merchant-stated comparison price, not an independently observed historical average.
- **5/20** sampled detail pages showed `No Disponible`/`En espera del producto`, including a shopper product [Nutri Snacks SKU 128263](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/128263/-nutri-snacks-granola-lite-cacao-almendras). The grid still lists these SKUs and prices, so catalog presence does **not** prove purchasability. Do not generalize 5/20 to an out-of-stock rate; the edge sample deliberately includes last/high-price SKUs.
- Shopper landing page said `Válido del 7 al 13 de Octubre 2026`, yet sampled detail pages such as [Charmin SKU 124586](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/124586/charmin-ultra-strong-mega-rolls) said `Válido hasta el 20 de octubre de 2026`; [chicken SKU 695852](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/695852/de-estados-unidos-muslos-de-pollo-jumbo-frescos) said October 13. A catalog-only multi-buy had no expiry displayed. Preserve per-product promo expiry separately; do not assume the shopper header supplies every item's validity.
- Identity risk: 1,214 normalized names recur across multiple SKUs; 1,010 have multiple size strings. **251** name+size groups still have multiple SKUs, and **131** of those groups have different displayed prices. For example [Bounty SKU 138018](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/138018/bounty-papel-toalla) (`2 CT`, `$4.99`, `Reg. $7.49`, D/R) versus [SKU 138016](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/138016/bounty-papel-toalla) (`2 CT`, `$7.99`, D/P). The shopper has four `PLANET OAT OATMILK CREAMER 32 OZ` SKUs at the same price, but product details distinguish vanilla, caramel, coffee cake, and brown sugar; one sampled flavor is unavailable. SKU must anchor Pueblo's product identity and Mi Lista matching, not `product_norm` alone. The catalog also contains 813 names beginning with `*`; [a catalog-only milk SKU 143009](https://www.puebloweb.com/productos/143009/-indulac-leche-2-reducida-en-grasa) has it without a `Reg.` label, so `*` is not a reliable promotion flag.
- Store/channel caveat: Pueblo's [FAQ](https://www.puebloweb.com/preguntas-frecuentes) asks customers to select a pickup store and notes substitutes/out-of-stock adjustments can change the final charge. The [terms](https://www.puebloweb.com/terminos) describe prices honored on PuebloWeb.com at order time. Treat these as *online displayed* prices; no audited evidence establishes identical prices or availability at every physical branch.
- Publication consideration, not a blocker to the pilot: Pueblo's [terms](https://www.puebloweb.com/terminos) ask for written permission to reproduce or publish its proprietary site material elsewhere. PLAZA's current intent is noncommercial comparison of publicly visible product names and prices, not copying Pueblo's images, branding or page design. The clause does not itself settle whether factual price data is covered. Continue technical testing; revisit the scope with appropriate legal advice or supermarket arrangements if substantial content republication or commercialization is contemplated.

## Verdict: PARTIAL for production use

SKU matching and deterministic capture work, but the detail pages expose distinct regular-price labels, extra non-shopper promotions, availability and item-specific expiry not present in the grid. The current PLAZA `product_norm`-keyed list/offer model would conflate some variants if all catalog rows were loaded unchanged. No production ingestion or user alert should be enabled from this pilot alone. Next technical step: test a *selective* detail fetch for watched and shopper SKUs (not 20,175 pages every run), model `shopper`, `other merchant promotion`, `regular/current`, `unknown`, and `unavailable` separately, and compare merchant `Reg.` against actual non-promo snapshots over time.
