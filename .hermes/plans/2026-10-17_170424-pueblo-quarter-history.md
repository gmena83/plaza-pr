## Definitions and non-negotiable boundaries

1. **Quarter target for Pueblo:** at least **14 consecutive** printed seven-day edition starts with a complete `type=shopper` and `type=all` snapshot, spanning **at least 90 days** from first to last start. October 7, 2026 → January 6, 2027 is 14 starts / 91 days *if every intervening edition is captured*. Twelve more editions are needed after October 14. A missed week moves the consecutive-series target forward; do not stitch across a gap or label 13 starts/84 days a full three months.
2. **Broader shopper-history target:** for each chain, report first/last trustworthy printed validity dates, expected 7/14/28/30-day publication cadence, captured editions and missing periods over a rolling ≥90-day window. Record exactly which chains satisfy that condition; do **not** claim 11-chain completeness from the shared database's oldest/newest date. Repeated extraction of one edition is not extra history.
3. Full online catalog prices are *not* verified regular/in-store prices or inventory. Shopper rows are advertised specials only. Local archived VL rows remain segregated until page-level audit and conservative identity matching. User lists, API, live `offers`, and the fictional projection must never be filled with incomplete study snapshots.
4. A 90-day corpus permits a **review**, not a prediction release. The existing evaluator's item-level error is insufficient for a personalized buy/wait claim; prospective, consented frozen baskets, missing-outcome accounting and an owner-approved decision threshold remain separate gates.
